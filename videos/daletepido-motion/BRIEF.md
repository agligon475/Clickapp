## Intent
Ultra-dynamic, high-contrast 3-way comparison motion graphics video (9:16 vertical ratio, 1080x1920, 1800 frames at 30fps / 60 seconds) for Dale!TePido.
Exposes the stark reality between 3 types of merchants:
- Store A: Delivery App Merchant (Third-party app, -35% commissions, delayed riders, zero customer control, ranking penalties).
- Store B: Offline Traditional Store (Faded, shutter half-down, paper flyers, 0 delivery, chaotic notebook & audio messages).
- Store C: Dale!TePido Smart Store (Architectural clean lines, vivid red canopy #F60000, 3D acrylic QR stand, own fleet with fixed rates, Google local SEO, direct WhatsApp, 0% commissions, 100% customer ownership).
